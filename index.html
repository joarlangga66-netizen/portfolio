01What do you need to get started?

Access to your QuickBooks Online or Xero file (as an invited user or accountant), your bank and credit card statements, and a short call to understand how your business works.

02Which software do you use?

QuickBooks Online and Xero. I'm QuickBooks Online Certified and a QuickBooks ProAdvisor (Silver). I also work with Double (formerly Keeper), Hubdoc, Bill.com, Gusto Payroll, ServiceTitan, Service Fusion, Jobber, AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi Voyager, DoorLoop and monday.com, plus Excel and Google Sheets for schedules and reports. I also use Claude and ChatGPT to speed up reviews, and I check every result by hand.

03Can you fix books that are behind?

Yes. Bookkeeping cleanup is one of my core services. I'll review the file, correct what's wrong and reconcile month by month until you're current.

04Is my financial data secure?

I work through the user access you grant inside your software, so passwords are never shared. My accounts use 2-factor authentication, and NDAs are welcome.

05What hours do you work?

I'm based in the Philippines (GMT+8) and can schedule calls and turnaround times around your business hours.

06How much do you charge?

It depends on your transaction volume, the number of accounts, and how current your books are. Book a free 30-minute call and I'll give you a clear monthly quote, with no surprises.

07How do we communicate?

Whatever works best for your team. I use email, Slack and WhatsApp for day-to-day questions, and Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams for regular check-ins. If your team already uses another tool, I'm happy to join it.
